        From activity to action

        Your LMS has the signals.
        PlugLytics makes them useful.

        Learning platforms record a lot: submissions, grades, attendance, course activity, and billing events. PlugLytics is designed to organize those signals into understandable insights instead of another pile of spreadsheets.

        ↗

        Spot support needs sooner

        Bring missed work, attendance patterns, and grade movement together so educators can follow up with context.

        ◎

        See what is happening in class

        Review assignment participation, quiz question difficulty, section-level grade patterns, and engagement timing.

        $

        Connect learning with operations

        Give authorized administrators a shared view of enrollment, billing, collections, balances, and academic outcomes.

        One platform, useful views

        Built for the people behind every learning outcome.

        01 / STUDENT
        ↗

        Track your progress.

        Students can check their curriculum map, unit completion, grade trends, attendance, and upcoming deadlines.

        • What needs attention this week?
        • How is my progress changing?
        • What support might help me stay on track?
        02 / INSTRUCTOR
        ⌁

        Teach with better context.

        Instructors can compare their own sections, review assignment submissions, find difficult quiz items, and see engagement patterns.

        • Which questions are confusing learners?
        • Where is participation dropping?
        • Which students may need a check-in?
        03 / ADMINISTRATOR
        ▤

        Make decisions with a wider view.

        Administrators can explore enrollment, seat utilization, retention, tuition collection, academic quality, and course demand.

        • Where are seats and resources being used?
        • What is billed, collected, or outstanding?
        • How do courses and terms compare?

        For teams building with PlugLytics

        Bring your analytics workflow into VS Code.

        Install the PlugLytics extension to open the dashboard and API docs, start the local API, check service health, and choose date filters from your editor. Its optional LMS JSON connector reads date fields only; course and activity data still need a provider-specific integration.
